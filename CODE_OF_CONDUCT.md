# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone.

Participants must use welcoming and inclusive language, respect differing viewpoints and experience, accept constructive feedback, focus on what is best for the community, and show empathy toward others. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, publication of private information, and other unprofessional conduct are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior they reasonably consider inappropriate. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid unnecessary disclosure of affected people.

Sensitive conduct reports may use this repository's GitHub private vulnerability-reporting form with the title prefixed `CONDUCT:`. Use that private route only when public reporting could expose or endanger a person; ordinary project disagreements belong in a public governance issue. Do not include unnecessary personal details. Maintainers will protect reporter confidentiality to the extent reasonably possible.
